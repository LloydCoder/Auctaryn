What counts as a "protected agent"?
Any agent_id that registers an identity and has its actions routed through the Execution Gateway. One AI agent in production, regardless of how many tool calls it makes per day, counts as one protected agent.
Is identity enforcement on by default?
No — it ships opt-in so it never breaks an existing deployment. You enable it once your agent fleet is registered. Circuit breaker isolation is on by default since it's purely protective.
Can I self-host?
Yes. The Community tier is Apache 2.0 and fully self-hostable. Paid tiers add managed infrastructure, SIEM export, and priority support — but nothing is locked behind a SaaS-only wall.
What's the difference between TwinGuard and OpenShell?
OpenShell sandboxes the agent's execution environment — filesystem, network, syscalls. TwinGuard protects what happens inside the agent's reasoning and decision loop, which OpenShell doesn't touch.
